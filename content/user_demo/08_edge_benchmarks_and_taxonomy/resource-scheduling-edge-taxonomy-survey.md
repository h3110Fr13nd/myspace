# Resource Scheduling in Edge Computing: Taxonomy & Comprehensive Survey

Resource allocation and task scheduling lie at the heart of Mobile Edge Computing (MEC), Fog Computing, and Edge AI systems. This survey presents a multi-dimensional taxonomy categorizing existing literature across four core axes: Infrastructure Architecture, Optimization Objectives, Solution Methodologies, and Evaluation Testbeds <Citation id="8" authors="P. Sharma et al." year="2026" title="Edge Scheduling Taxonomy" />.

<Callout type="important" title="Taxonomy Structural Axes">
1. **Infrastructure Topologies**: Single-Edge Server, Multi-Access Edge Mesh, Cloud-Edge-Fog Continuum, Vehicular RSU Networks.
2. **Primary Optimization Targets**: End-to-End Latency, Energy Dissipation, Cloud Monetary Cost, Transmission Reliability, Service Availability.
3. **Algorithmic Families**: Exact Mathematical Programming (MILP), Heuristics (HEFT, CPOP), Metaheuristics (PSO, GA, ACO), Deep Reinforcement Learning (DQN, PPO, SAC).
</Callout>
