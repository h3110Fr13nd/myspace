This public folder is a small, living demo. Open the **graph view** (top right) while you read: every link below is an edge, and the yellow ones are *named relationships*.

> [!tip] Try this
> Hover a yellow link in the expanded graph to read its name, or hover a chip in **Linked references** at the bottom of a note.

## What to look at

1. **Named relationships** — [[showcase-knowledge-graphs|Knowledge graphs|introduces]] explains the syntax.
2. **The streamed graph** — [[showcase-streaming-tiles|Streaming tiles|introduces]] shows how a million nodes stay smooth.
3. **Canvases** — [[showcase-canvas-at-scale|Canvas at scale|introduces]] shows why big boards stay smooth.
4. **Open questions** — [[showcase-open-questions]] is a plain link, for contrast.

## Writing features

| Feature | Write | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Wikilink | `[[note]]` | a link and a graph edge |
| Alias | `[[note\|shown text]]` | the link reads *shown text* |
| Relationship | `[[note\|shown\|cites]]` | a named, yellow edge |
| Math | `$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$` | $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ |
